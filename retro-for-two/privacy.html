Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026  |  Version 1.0

Retro for Two ("the App") is published by Holistic Governance Pty Ltd (ABN 52 698 725 558) ("we", "us"). We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

The short version: everything you enter in Retro for Two stays on your phone. We don't run a server for the App, there are no accounts, and we can't see your retros.

1. What the App stores, and where

The following is stored only on your device, in the App's private storage:

  • The names you enter for you and your partner
  • Your retro schedule and reminder settings
  • Check-in scores, agreed actions and appreciations
  • Written answers, if you choose to keep them. By default they are deleted when each retro ends.
  • Any custom questions you add

Because this information never leaves your device, we do not collect, access, use or disclose it. Content about your relationship can be sensitive; you control it entirely.

2. Deleting your information

You can delete a single retro, clear written answers, or erase everything from within the App (Settings → Erase everything). Uninstalling the App also deletes its data. Device backups (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include App data according to your device's backup settings.

3. Purchases

If you buy Retro for Two Pro, payment is processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to confirm your purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic device information (such as platform and app version). It never receives your retro content. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Purchase records may be stored outside Australia (for example in the United States).

4. Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification server is involved. Reminder wording is deliberately neutral because notifications can appear on your lock screen.

5. What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of personal information
  • No automated decision-making about you

6. App lock

If you turn on App lock, your device's Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint system checks your identity. The App never receives your biometric data.

7. Children

Retro for Two is designed for adults in relationships and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date, and note the change in the App's release notes.

9. Contact and complaints

Email support@hg-au.com. Holistic Governance Pty Ltd, Victoria, Australia.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner: www.oaic.gov.au, 1300 363 992.

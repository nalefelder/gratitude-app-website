Retro for Two

Regular, kind check-ins for a healthier relationship

Retro for Two helps couples hold a short, structured conversation on a regular rhythm. You each answer a few questions privately, talk them through together, agree on one to three small things to try, and finish with appreciation.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. There's no account and no cloud, so we can't see your retros. Deleting the app deletes its data, apart from any device backup you've made (for example iCloud or Google backup).

Why did my written answers disappear?

By default, written answers are cleared when a retro ends; check-in scores, agreed actions and appreciations are kept. You can choose to keep answers in Settings → Privacy.

Can we use it on two phones?

Retros are designed for one phone, passed between you. Either partner can also use "Just me" mode on their own phone to prepare notes.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

How do I erase everything?

Settings → Erase everything. This can't be undone.

If a conversation doesn't feel safe

Retro for Two is a conversation tool, not therapy. If you feel afraid of your partner, please skip the retro and reach out for support.
